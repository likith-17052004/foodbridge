Google AI Builder Cup · Bengaluru prototype

One photo of leftover food → a matched NGO pickup, before the food spoils.

Restaurants throw away edible food every night while orphanages, old-age homes and shelters nearby run short. FoodBridge lets a busy kitchen list surplus with a single photo. Gemini works out what it is and how long it stays safe, and the offer goes to the NGO that can collect and serve it in time.

    How it works

    Every donation goes through the same six steps. The Google service doing the work is tagged on each.

    What's real and what's simulated

    Real20 NGOs (OpenStreetMap care homes, NGO Darpan registry), 40 Bengaluru restaurants with closing times (Zomato), census wards, Routes API travel times, Gemini analysis of every photo, USDA/FSSAI safety rules, FAO CO₂e factor.
    SimulatedEach NGO's capacity and daily meal gap, how much surplus each kitchen usually has, and the 45 nights of donation history behind the dashboard and forecast. Simulated fields are labelled sim_ in the data.
    Demo onlyIn the control-room rush, NGO replies are automatic, offers expire after 20 s instead of 10 min, and pickups are shown as a time-lapse. Rush pickups are kept out of the impact numbers.

    Good to know

    • First load can take a few seconds. The app scales to zero when idle to keep costs near zero, then wakes on the first visit.
    • Veg / non-veg uses two keys. Gemini proposes and the kitchen confirms before food can go to a veg-only NGO. Gemini's own confidence didn't reliably flag its mistakes, so a human check stays in the loop.
    • Daily AI limit. This personal prototype caps Gemini calls per day. If you see "daily limit reached", the rest of the app still works with existing donations.
    • Sample dishes are photos of a single plate, so the batch size is pre-filled at 25 servings, as a kitchen would confirm.
    • Want to play the NGO? In the control room, untick "NGOs auto-respond" and use Accept / Decline yourself.
