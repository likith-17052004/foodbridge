How the forecast works

  • Every listed donation is logged with place, time and servings (BigQuery in production).
  • Per ~3 km area: a weekday-aware average of past nights (60%) blended with the last 14 nights (40%), plus the area's hour-of-day profile.
  • For each busy area, NGOs that can reach it within 35 min at the peak hour are suggested for standby, ranked by need.
  • Accuracy is measured on the last 7 completed nights, each predicted only from earlier nights.
  • Production: the same features feed a Vertex AI / BigQuery ML forecasting model once real pickups accumulate.
